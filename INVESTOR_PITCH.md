# Uzb Beauty Find - Investor Overview

## Executive Summary
**Uzb Beauty Find** is disrupting the beauty service industry in Uzbekistan by creating the first comprehensive digital marketplace that connects beauty professionals with clients through a gamified, trustworthy, and efficient platform.

## The Problem
1.  **Fragmentation**: Finding high-quality salons and masters is difficult; clients rely on word-of-mouth or scattered Instagram pages.
2.  **Trust Issue**: "Instagram vs. Reality" is a consistent problem. Clients cannot verify the quality of service before visiting.
3.  **Booking Friction**: Scheduling requires phone calls and DMs, leading to lost time and missed appointments.
4.  **Business Inefficiency**: Salons lack modern tools to manage clients, fill empty slots, and market their services effectively.

## The Solution
A unified platform that serves as a **Booking Engine**, **Marketing Tool**, and **Trust Anchor**.

### For Clients
-   **Smart Search**: Find salons by location, service, price, and rating.
-   **Trust Score & Verified Reviews**: Review system requiring verified bookings to prevent fake feedback.
-   **Gamified Loyalty**: Earn coins, participate in lotteries, and win free services.
-   **Instant Booking**: 24/7 real-time booking without phone calls.

### For Businesses
-   **Digital Storefront**: Professional profile with portfolio, pricing, and master details.
-   **Smart Marketing**: "Last Minute" deals to fill empty slots and "Lotteries" to attract new customers.
-   **Management Dashboard**: Unified view of bookings, client history, and financial performance.

## Key Differentiators
1.  **Trust-First Architecture**: Our "Trust Score" algorithm incentivizes reliable behavior for both clients (showing up) and masters (quality service).
2.  **Gamification**: Unlike boring directories, we engage users with "Coins", raffles, and dynamic promotions, increasing retention.
3.  **Local Focus & Scale**: Tailored specifically for the Central Asian market nuances (language, payment habits) with architecture ready for rapid expansion.

## Revenue Model
1.  **Subscription Saas**: Monthly fees for Salons for premium dashboard features.
2.  **Commission**: Small fee on high-value bookings or "Last Minute" fills.
3.  **Promoted Listings**: Salons pay to appear at the top of search results.
4.  **Coin Economy**: Users purchase coins for premium entry to exclusive lotteries or gifts.

## Technology Status
-   **MVP Ready**: Core booking, search, and review flows are implemented.
-   **Scalable Stack**: Built on React/Vite and Supabase (PostgreSQL), ready to handle thousands of concurrent users.
-   **Mobile First**: Optimized for the mobile web, ensuring access for the 90%+ mobile user base in the region.
